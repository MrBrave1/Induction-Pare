🚨 Committed to No Harm

"No one gets hurt – That's the goal."
At ANDRITZ, excellence begins with Health and Safety. We protect everyone we impact — employees, contractors, and visitors.

Safety is not separate from work — it is built into everything we do. Safety comes before short-term targets or pressure. We speak up, take action, and look out for each other.

What this means for everyone on site:

  • Leaders prioritize safety in every decision, even under pressure.
  • Supervisors set expectations, give guidance, and stop unsafe work.
  • Every person on site follows the rules and looks out for their team.
CEO Commitment: "A bad financial result can be recovered, but harm to someone's life cannot be undone."

⚠️ General Safety Rules

  • No drugs, alcohol, or unauthorized smoking.
  • Stop work immediately if it is unsafe.
  • Work only if trained, competent, and authorized.
  • Follow all site safety and environmental rules.
  • If unsure, stop and ask your supervisor.
Remember: Safety is everyone's responsibility. Always work safely, follow procedures, and report unsafe conditions immediately.

✅ Do's

  • If you are a Doctor or First Aider, please inform the Site Manager/HSE Advisor.
  • Follow all safety instructions and warning signs displayed in the work areas.
  • Use appropriate PPE as required for the area being visited.
  • Watch your step and avoid tripping over materials, tools, or other objects.

❌ Don'ts

  • Do not enter the powerhouse unless authorized by Security.
  • Do not enter excavations, confined spaces, or approach hazardous materials.
  • Do not touch or operate any equipment, materials, or chemicals.
  • Do not carry cameras or recording devices without prior permission.
  • Smoking and consumption of tobacco/gutka is strictly prohibited.
